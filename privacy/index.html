Privacy Policy

Sprindle keeps your account and your taste on your phone. It listens only when you tap Identify, and never keeps the sound. We don't run ads, track you across apps, or sell anything about you.

01Who we are

Sprindle helps you discover records, the producers and labels behind them, and the shops that sell them. This policy explains what information the Sprindle app handles and why. "We" means Sprindle, the operator of the app.

02What stays on your device

When you create an account you give us a name, an email address and a password. These are stored only on your device, together with everything that makes Sprindle yours:

• the styles you pick, and the producers and labels you follow
• the records you save, and the label catalogue records you mark as owned or liked
• your Daily Dig: the records picked for you and what you kept or skipped
• your Recent Finds: the songs you've identified and the records they're on
• your notification settings, and which new records you've already been told about

The app also keeps copies of the catalogue, the profiles and release pages you've opened, and their sleeves and photos, so it opens quickly and works offline. These are the same for everyone and say nothing about you.

The Daily Dig and drop alerts are worked out on your device from the catalogue everyone downloads, so who you follow and what you like are never sent to us or anyone else. If you browse without an account, the same applies to what you do as a guest.

Your email address and password are never sent to our servers. Your password is never stored as you typed it; the app keeps a salted one-way hash so it can check it when you sign in. Your name is sent only if you redeem a promo code, as explained under Event tickets.

03What our servers see

The app asks our servers for what it shows:

• the Sprindle catalogue: new releases, label catalogues, artist profiles and which shops have a record
• an artist's discography when you open their profile, and a release's tracklist when you open it
• record sleeves and artist photos
• the record a song is on, when you identify one (see Identify)

None of these requests carries your name, email address or account, and the catalogue is the same download for everyone. Like any web server, ours sees your IP address and the time of each request. Our request logs keep only the kind of request and the page asked for: not your IP address, and not the songs you identify. Our hosting provider may keep connection records briefly for security. We don't use any of this to identify you.

04Identify

When you tap Identify, Sprindle uses your iPhone's microphone for up to 20 seconds to recognise the song that's playing, with Apple's ShazamKit. The sound is turned into an audio fingerprint on your device, and only the fingerprint is sent to Apple to find the match; Apple's privacy policy applies to that request. Sprindle never records, uploads or keeps the sound itself, and the microphone is on only while the screen says it's listening. Leaving the screen stops it at once.

To find the record a song is on, the app sends the song's title, artist and ISRC (a code that identifies the recording) to our servers. They look it up in Sprindle's own catalogue first, then in MusicBrainz. That request carries no account information, and we keep no record of who identified what.

The songs you identify are kept on your device as Recent Finds. You can remove one with a long press, or clear them all at any time.

05Notifications

Sprindle only sends notifications you turn on, and you can turn them off at any time in Profile or in your iPhone's Settings.

• The Daily Dig reminder is scheduled on your device for each morning.
• Drop alerts tell you about new records from the producers and labels you follow. To catch them, the app may refresh the catalogue in the background when iOS allows it, using the same download described above.

Both are created by the app on your device. We don't use a push notification service, so we never hold a device token or know who has alerts turned on.

06Event tickets

When you redeem a promo code, our servers store the name on your account with the ticket, so it can be shown on the ticket and checked at the door, along with when you were checked in. If you invite a friend, the person who invited them can see their name once they redeem the invite. Your email address is never sent. Tickets are kept until after the event, then deleted.

07Services from other companies

• Songs are recognised by Apple's ShazamKit, as described under Identify.
• Apple Music previews are streamed directly from Apple when you press play. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request.
• Record sleeves and artist photos come from Apple Music, the Cover Art Archive (hosted by the Internet Archive) and Wikimedia Commons. Most are copies kept on our servers; a few, such as large sleeves on a record's page, load straight from the source. Those requests carry no information about you.
• Maps of record shops are provided by Apple Maps.
• If you share a label card, it goes through your iPhone's share sheet to the app you choose; nothing is sent to us.
• Links to Apple Music, Discogs, Resident Advisor, shop websites and ticket pages open those services, whose own policies apply once you're there.

Shop stock and prices come from the shops themselves and from Discogs; catalogue details and discographies from MusicBrainz; artist biographies from Wikipedia and Discogs. Our servers fetch these on their own schedule, and no information about you is shared with any of them.

08What we don't do

Sprindle has no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. It doesn't access your location, contacts, photos or camera, and uses the microphone only while you're identifying a song. We don't sell, rent or share personal information.

09Keeping or deleting your data

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. That erases from the device your account, the producers and labels you follow, your saved, owned and liked records, your Daily Dig history, your Recent Finds, your event tickets, and cancels any Sprindle notifications. Deleting the app removes everything as well.

A redeemed ticket is also kept on our servers until after the event, then deleted. If you'd like yours deleted sooner, email us at mert.deryaasan@gmail.com.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because your account lives on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app. For an event ticket, or anything else, email us at mert.deryaasan@gmail.com.

11Children

Sprindle is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where local law sets a higher age, and we do not knowingly handle their information.

12Changes to this policy

If we change how Sprindle handles information, for example when accounts start syncing between devices, we'll update this policy and the date above, and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
