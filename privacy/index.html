Privacy Policy

Sprindle keeps your account on your phone. We don't run ads, track you across apps, or sell anything about you.

01Who we are

Sprindle helps you discover records, the producers who make them and the shops that sell them. This policy explains what information the Sprindle app handles and why. "We" means Sprindle, the operator of the app.

02What stays on your device

When you create an account you give us a name, an email address and a password. Along with the styles you pick, the producers you follow and the records you save, these are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers.

Your password is never stored as you typed it; the app keeps a salted one-way hash so it can check your password when you sign in.

03What our servers see

To show new releases, previews and which shops have a record, the app downloads the Sprindle catalog from our servers. That request carries no account information. Like any web server, ours can see your IP address and the time of the request, and may keep them briefly in logs for security and troubleshooting. We don't use them to identify you.

04Services from other companies

• Apple Music previews are streamed directly from Apple when you press play. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request.
• Maps of record shops are provided by Apple Maps.
• Links to Apple Music, Discogs, Resident Advisor, shop websites and ticket pages open those services, whose own policies apply once you're there.

Shop stock and prices come from the shops themselves and from Discogs. No information about you is shared with them.

05What we don't do

Sprindle has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not access your location, contacts, photos or microphone. We don't sell or rent personal information.

06Keeping or deleting your data

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account, which erases your account, follows and saved records from the device. Deleting the app removes everything as well.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because your account lives on your device, you can exercise these directly in the app. For anything else, email us at mert.deryaasan@gmail.com.

08Children

Sprindle is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where local law sets a higher age, and we do not knowingly handle their information.

09Changes to this policy

If we change how Sprindle handles information, for example when accounts start syncing between devices, we'll update this policy and the date above, and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.

10Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at mert.deryaasan@gmail.com.
